For almost 65 years, Eden Valley has been a place of ministry, growth, service, and preparation and God has done remarkable things here.

We have trained missionaries to carry the gospel to the world. We have welcomed guests into our lifestyle program, and thousands of lives have been touched by healing and restoration of health. But more than physical healing, souls have met the Great Physician here.

None of that happens without the faithful staff and volunteer families who give their lives to this work. They cook, clean, teach, counsel, maintain, mentor, and pray often behind the scenes, and often in very tight living spaces.

It has been about 40 years since ground was broken for new staff housing here at Eden Valley. Our families have faithfully made do, in cramped, aging spaces, but we have outgrown what we have. These are the people who give everything so that others can be healed and sent out.

That is why in 2023, we launched a new staff housing project: a master plan to build eight homes for our volunteer families. By God's grace, we have already built three duplexes now filled with families serving here. Several uninhabitable trailers have been removed to make way for the plan. The progress is real, but the work is not finished.
