The story so far

From Worn Trailers to Welcoming Homes

“Except the LORD build the house, they labour in vain that build it.”Psalm 127:1
  1. 1

    Where the story begins

    A worn trailer stands alone in a dry field, a quiet reminder of how long our families have waited.

  2. 2

    Making room

    The old comes down, so that something better can rise in its place.

  3. 3

    After about 40 years, the earth is opened once more for new staff housing.
